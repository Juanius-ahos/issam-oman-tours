Things to do in Muscat

Updated November 14, 2026

Muscat is spread along the coast between the mountains and the sea, and its best places are easy to combine in one day. Here are the places most travelers come for, and how to see them.

Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque

The country’s great mosque, with its huge prayer hall, chandelier and hand-woven carpet. It opens to non-Muslim visitors on weekday mornings, so it is usually the first stop of the day.

Dress code: women cover their hair, and everyone wears long sleeves and long trousers or a long skirt.

Mutrah Corniche and Souq

The seafront promenade along the old harbour, and one of the oldest souqs in the region behind it: frankincense, silver, textiles and karak tea. It is at its best in the late afternoon and evening.

Old Muscat and Al Alam Palace

The ceremonial palace with its blue and gold facade, between the Al Jalali and Al Mirani forts above the bay. The palace and forts are seen from outside, and the area is small enough to walk.

Royal Opera House Muscat

A white stone opera house in Shati Al Qurum, worth seeing by day and lit up at night. Check the program if you want to attend a performance.

Muscat by night

When the heat drops, Muscat comes alive: the lit Opera House, the Corniche full of families and the evening souq. Issam runs this as a private evening tour.

Day trips from Muscat

The capital is also the base for day trips: Nizwa and its fort, the Wahiba Sands, Wadi Shab on the coast with the Bimmah Sinkhole on the way, and the mud-brick village of Al Hamra.

Tips

  • Start early: mornings are cooler, and the Grand Mosque is open to visitors in the morning.
  • Dress modestly in the old town and souqs: shoulders and knees covered.
  • Cards work in most places; keep some cash for taxis and small shops.
  • A half-day tour (8:00 to 12:00) covers the main sights; a full day (8:00 to 15:00) leaves time for the hidden corners.

Quick answers

How many days do you need in Muscat?
One full day covers the Grand Mosque, Mutrah and Old Muscat. Add an evening for Muscat by night, and use Muscat as a base for day trips.
What should I wear to the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque?
Women cover their hair, and everyone wears long sleeves and long trousers or a long skirt.
Is Muscat walkable?
Each area, such as Mutrah or Old Muscat, is easy to walk, but the city is long and spread out, so you need transport between them.
