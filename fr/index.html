Questions

Before you message

How much does a private tour cost?
Placeholder answer. Prices depend on the tour, the group size and the season. Send your dates and group size on WhatsApp and you get a clear price for the whole group, with no per-head surprises.
Where do you pick us up?
Placeholder answer. From any hotel, apartment or cruise terminal in Muscat. Pickup from other cities can be arranged.
How long are the tours?
Placeholder answer. Muscat city is a half or full day, Nizwa and Wadi Shab are full days, and the Wahiba desert is an overnight trip. Every tour can be shortened or extended.
Which languages do you guide in?
Placeholder answer. Arabic plus English, Spanish, Italian, German, French, Chinese, Portuguese, Catalan, Russian, Korean, Japanese and Hindi. Mixed-language groups are welcome.
How big can the group be?
Placeholder answer. Tours are private, from one traveler up to six in one vehicle. Larger groups can travel with a second vehicle.
How do we book and pay?
Placeholder answer. Message on WhatsApp or send the request form. A small deposit secures the date and the rest is paid on the day.
